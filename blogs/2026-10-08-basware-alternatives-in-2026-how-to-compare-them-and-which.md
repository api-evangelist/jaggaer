If you only need AP automation, compare Basware with enterprise AP platforms such as Esker and Medius, or stay on Basware. If you need strategic sourcing and contracts governed with purchasing, you need a source-to-pay platform instead. Enterprise procurement software is now judged on how well it controls spend from the sourcing event onward.
